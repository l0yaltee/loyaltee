IlvolAI ("we", "us") makes the IlvolAI desktop app for Windows, which listens to
freight broker calls and turns them into structured load records. This policy
explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Contact: support@loyaltee.uz

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address and password (stored hashed by our
authentication provider, never in readable form). If you belong to a team
account, we also store your role and the email address you were invited with.

**Call audio (processed, not stored).** When you start a capture, the app
records audio from your microphone and/or the audio playing on your computer.
That audio is streamed live to our speech-to-text provider to produce a
transcript. We do not save audio recordings on our servers or on your device.
The app captures audio only while a capture session is running.

**Transcripts and load records.** The transcript of each call, and the load
details extracted from it (pickup and delivery locations and times, commodity,
equipment, rate, weight, notes, and similar), are stored in your account so you
can view, edit and export them. If you use call scoring, we also store the
score, which steps were completed, short quotes from the transcript that
justify them, and, for lost loads, the reason.

**Usage data.** For each capture we record counts and durations (such as
capture sessions, audio seconds and processing volume) to enforce plan limits
and calculate costs. If you are a member of a company, this usage is also
counted against the company's shared allowance. We keep this usage record
permanently, because consumed usage cannot be given back. We do not use
advertising or analytics trackers.

**Billing records.** When you buy a plan or top-up, we store the plan, amount,
status and the payment provider's transaction identifiers. We never receive or
store your card number; payment is completed on the payment provider's side.
If you pay by Click invoice, we store the phone number the invoice was sent to.

**Technical session data.** To enforce how many devices may be signed in to an
account at once, we record which sessions have been replaced, kept for 30 days.

## 2. How we use it

- To provide the app: transcribe calls, extract loads, show your history.
- To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- To apply plan limits, process payments and prevent abuse.
- To measure cost and improve reliability.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your calls or transcripts to
advertise to you.

## 3. Who processes your data

We use these service providers only to run IlvolAI:

- **Supabase** – hosting, database and authentication.
- **Deepgram** – speech-to-text. Your call audio is sent to Deepgram live while
  a capture is running.
- **Ollama (cloud-hosted language model)** – receives call transcripts to
  extract load details and score calls.
- **Payme and Click** – payment processing for plans and top-ups, for users who
  pay through them.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy
policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

## 4. Call recording and other people's consent

IlvolAI captures audio of calls you take. Laws on recording and transcribing
calls differ by country and state, and some require that every party consent.
You are responsible for informing the other participants and obtaining any
consent the law requires before you capture a call.

## 5. Team accounts

If you use IlvolAI through an organization, the organization's owners and
admins can see usage and load records for the team and manage members. Your
organization's own policies also apply to that data.

Loads, transcripts and scores captured for an organization are the
organization's records. If you leave the organization, are removed from it, or
delete your account, those records stay with the organization in full. They may
keep the email address of the member who captured them, so the organization can
see who handled each call. The usage you consumed also stays counted against the
organization's allowance. This is described in our Terms of Service.

## 6. Retention

We keep account, load and transcript data for as long as your account exists.
Billing records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal
obligations. Replaced-session records are deleted after 30 days.

When you delete your account, we delete your login and your personal data:
loads and transcripts you captured outside any organization, your sessions and
memberships, and your per-user usage totals. We keep, without a link to you:
payment and subscription records (as required for accounting; your email and
phone number are removed from them), and usage records (counts only, no content).
Loads captured for an organization stay with the organization as described
above, together with the email address of the member who captured them. Any
unused prepaid balance and the remainder of a paid period are forfeited on
deletion.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit and archive your loads in the app. You can delete your
account yourself in the app, under Settings, "Delete account". If you own an
organization that still has other members, remove them first. You can also ask
us at any time to access, correct, export or delete your data by writing to
support@loyaltee.uz; we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live,
you may have additional rights under local law, including the right to object
to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority.

## 8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access
controls so that each user can reach only their own or their organization's
records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

## 9. Children

IlvolAI is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not
knowingly collect their data.

## 10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the
United States and the European Union. We rely on those providers' safeguards
for such transfers.

## 11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for material
changes we will notify you in the app or by email.
